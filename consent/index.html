Before youplay

Every player at Xtreme Arena signs a consent and waiver once. It takes about two minutes. Players under 18 need a parent or legal guardian to sign for them.

What we ask for, and why:

  • Your name, date of birth and contact details, so we know who is on the track.
  • An emergency contact, in case we need to reach someone quickly.
  • Anything you tell us about medical conditions, so our marshals can look out for you.
  • Your signature, which is what makes this consent valid.
