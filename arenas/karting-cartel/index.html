KARTINGCARTEL

Wheel to wheel, lap after lap. The fastest way to find out what you are made of.

The Karting Cartel is where the noise starts. Purpose-built karts, a circuit that rewards a clean line over brute throttle, and timing that does not care how good you think you are.

Race solo against the clock or bring a group and settle it properly. Marshals brief every session, so first-timers and regulars share the same grid safely.

Good toknow

  • Timed sessions with live lap boards
  • Race-prepared karts and full safety gear provided
  • Group and corporate race formats
  • Marshal briefing before every session
