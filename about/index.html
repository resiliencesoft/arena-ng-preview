AboutXtreme Arena

India's Most Extreme Adventure Arena. Unleash the thrill. Live the adrenaline.

Built forchampions

Xtreme Arena was built on a simple idea: that the best days out are the ones you are still talking about a week later.

So we put twelve arenas on one site. Karting and motorbikes for the people who measure a good day in lap times. Paintball and team combat for the ones who would rather win it together. A zipline and a jump tower for anyone who needs the ground to be further away. Turf, courts, targets and bows for everyone else.

Every arena is staffed, briefed and equipped before anyone sets foot in it, because the fastest way to ruin a thrill is to get the safety wrong.
